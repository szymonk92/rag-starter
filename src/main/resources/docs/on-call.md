# On-call
Every product team runs a one-week on-call rotation that starts on Monday at 10:00 Warsaw time.
The primary must acknowledge a page within 15 minutes; if not, PagerDuty escalates to the secondary, then to the engineering manager.
On-call engineers receive a flat allowance of 600 PLN per week plus 150 PLN for each night page answered between 22:00 and 07:00.
After a night with more than two pages, the engineer may start the next working day at noon.
