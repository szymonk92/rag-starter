# Expenses
Conference tickets up to 2,500 PLN are approved by your manager; above that the director must approve.
Each engineer has a yearly learning budget of 4,000 PLN for books, courses and certifications.
Submit receipts in Expensify within 30 days; late claims are not reimbursed.
Home-office equipment is reimbursed once every three years up to 3,000 PLN.
