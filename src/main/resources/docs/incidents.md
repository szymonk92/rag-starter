# Incidents
Severity 1 means customers cannot pay or log in; severity 2 means a degraded core feature; severity 3 is everything else.
For a SEV1 the incident commander opens a Slack channel named inc-<date>-<slug> and posts updates every 30 minutes.
A blameless postmortem is due within five working days for SEV1 and SEV2, and action items get a Jira owner and due date.
